# Cancellation Policy
_Last update 22nd July 2025_  
We hope that you look forward to receiving your purchases from us. However, if you change your mind before receiving your item(s) you may contact us to request order cancellation and a refund. Please see below for more information on our cancellation policy.
## CANCELLATIONS
All cancellations must be requested with time to process your cancellation. After your order has left the store we will be unable to cancel your order, however you can request to return your product(s) once they have arrived. Please see our [return policy](/refunds) for more information.
## CANCELLATION PROCESS
To request a cancellation, please email customer service at:  
[support@thisshopissogay.com](mailto:support@thisshopissogay.com)  
## REFUNDS
After receiving your cancellation request, we will process your refund. Please allow at least seven (7) days from confirmation of your cancellation to process your refund.

## EXCEPTIONS
We may not be able to cancel your order if it has already left the shop. In this eventuality, please see our [refund and return policy](/refunds) policy.

## QUESTIONS
If you have any questions concerning our cancellation policy, please contact us at:  
  
[support@thisshopissogay.com](mailto:support@thisshopissogay.com)
